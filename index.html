About

Who I am

I'm a fourth-year Computer Science and Information Systems student at Zagazig University, specializing in AI and Machine Learning. Alongside my studies, I founded Electregy, an AI-powered energy management platform for small commercial businesses in Egypt, built around a four-agent LLM pipeline for practical, Arabic-aware advising.

My technical foundation spans classical ML (XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost), deep learning (CNNs, RNN/LSTM, transfer learning), and generative AI (multi-agent pipelines, RAG fundamentals). I care about shipping work that's actually usable — production-structured code, clear documentation, and live, deployed apps rather than notebooks that never leave a laptop.

As AI Co-Lead at ZagEng, I mentor 50+ students, and I recently co-founded Robo Academy to teach programming fundamentals to secondary-school students. I'm currently training on the Agentic & Generative AI track through Egypt's Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI).
